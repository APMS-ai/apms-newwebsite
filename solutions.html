The problem

Every machine speaks
a language of its own

Machines were never built to talk to each other, so the numbers your floor produces stay locked inside each one.

Data trapped inside every machine

CNC, PLC and sensor each hold their own numbers in their own format, with no shared language and no way out unless someone builds one.

Numbers copied by hand, too late to use

A supervisor walks the floor, writes counts on paper, and someone re-types them into a spreadsheet. By the time anyone reads it, the shift is over.

A separate island for every vendor

Each machine builder ships its own portal and its own login, so the plant ends up with a dozen disconnected views and no single picture of the floor.

Older assets assumed unconnectable

A machine bought before controllers reported data gets written off as too old to monitor, so it runs unseen while newer lines get all the visibility.
