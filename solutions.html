Product tour

Four moves, one loop:
monitor, analyse, predict, act

The APMS loop never stops: every second of machine signal is captured, understood, forecast and acted on, then fed back in.

See every machine, every second

Every asset reports its own status, whether it is running, idle, stopped or in setup, read straight from the controller with nothing for anyone to write down. OEE and utilisation build themselves from that same feed, so the whole plant sits in one view and the shift ends without any guesswork.

Every loss traced to its cause

Downtime is tagged with its reason the moment it happens and ranked by what it actually costs, so recurring losses stop hiding in spreadsheets. You can cut the same picture by line or by shift, and watch MTBF and MTTR move as the fixes land.

Catch failures before they cost a shift

Vibration, load, temperature and cycle signatures feed anomaly detection, so wear and drift surface as an early warning rather than a breakdown. The same signals forecast tool life and usage, which turns maintenance into something you schedule instead of something that happens to you.

The right person, the right second

Alerts and escalations fire the moment reality drifts from plan, reaching the right person for the job on their phone or the dashboard in front of them. The same events push into your ERP, MES, email and SMS, so the response starts without anyone relaying a message.

What you can do

Four modules, one connected view

Production, downtime, machine health and quality: each captured live from the floor, each configurable to how your teams actually work.

Module 01

Production Monitoring

Track output against target in real time, every part, every job, every shift. See run-rate and throughput as they happen, so you know whether you'll hit the number long before the shift ends.

Tracked live
  • Good vs reject part counts
  • Target vs actual output
  • Cycle time: ideal vs actual
  • Throughput / parts per hour
  • Run rate & production pace
  • Job / work-order progress
  • On-time-delivery risk
  • Shift, line & operator comparison
Module 02

Downtime Tracking

Capture every stop automatically, classified by reason the moment it happens. Recurring losses stop hiding in spreadsheets: they surface, ranked by cost, so you fix the ones that actually matter.

Tracked live
  • Automatic stop start / end capture
  • Reason codes: tool change, material wait, setup, breakdown
  • Planned vs unplanned downtime
  • Downtime Pareto & top loss drivers
  • MTBF (mean time between failures)
  • MTTR (mean time to repair)
  • Response & acknowledgement time
  • Downtime by machine, shift & reason
Module 03

Machine Health & Condition

Read the vital signs of every asset directly from its controller and sensors. Catch wear, overheating and abnormal load before they become a breakdown, and move from reactive repairs to predictive maintenance.

Monitored parameters
  • Spindle load & motor current
  • Temperature: spindle, motor, ambient
  • Vibration & abnormal signatures
  • Hydraulic & pneumatic pressure
  • Coolant level & flow
  • Tool life & usage counts
  • Cycle & power-on hours
  • Anomaly & predictive-maintenance alerts
Module 04

Quality Monitoring

Tie quality to the machine and the moment it was made. Track scrap, rework and first-pass yield live and trace each to its cause, with SPC trends and an optional feed from Vision AI for 100% inline inspection.

Tracked live
  • First-pass yield (FPY)
  • Scrap & rework rate
  • Defect reason codes & reject Pareto
  • In-spec % against tolerance
  • SPC trends & control limits
  • Quality by machine, shift & part
  • Vision AI defect feed (optional)
  • Audit-ready quality records

Beyond monitoring

Maintenance & job management,
built in

Monitoring tells you something is wrong. This is the half that fixes it. A stop detected on the floor becomes a work order, an assignment and a closed job with its history intact, without anyone re-typing it into a second system.

Step 01

Signal

A stop, an alarm or a condition threshold is captured the moment it happens, straight from the controller.

Step 02

Raise

It becomes a work order automatically, tagged to the machine, the reason and the shift that saw it.

Step 03

Assign

The right technician gets it by role, with the checklist, spare parts and history already attached.

Step 04

Fix

Work is logged on the floor from a phone or panel. No paper, no end-of-shift catch-up.

Step 05

Learn

Every job feeds MTBF, MTTR and cost, so the next schedule is built on what actually happened.

PM · BDM

Maintenance Management

Preventive work runs on time, cycles or live condition, so a machine is serviced when it needs it. Breakdowns are captured as they happen with the downtime already attributed, and the condition trace below is what raises the next work order.

  • Time / cycle / condition schedules
  • Auto downtime capture
  • Work orders & assignments
  • MTBF / MTTR & cost
Job sheets · work orders

Digital Job Sheeting

  • Create in a few taps
  • Assign to machine / operator
  • Auto-linked to production
  • Searchable & exportable
