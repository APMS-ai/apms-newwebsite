The fleet

A unit for each kind of job

Bipedal

Humanoid

Works where people already work

Doors, stairs, walkways and bench heights are all built around a person, so a two-legged frame reaches the places a wheeled unit cannot. It picks up the repetitive handling and fetch-and-carry jobs on the shopfloor without anyone rebuilding the floor around it.

Four-legged

Quadruped

Goes where wheels give up

Gantries, cable trays, gratings, kerbs and wet or uneven floor stop a wheeled platform. Four legs keep a steady sensor payload over all of it, which is what makes an unmanned round of the plant possible at the hours nobody wants to walk it.

Autonomous mobile robot

AMR

Moves material without a driver

Trolleys, totes and work-in-progress between stations, on a route it plans itself around whatever is in the aisle that shift. No floor magnets, no guide tape and no line marking to maintain when the layout changes.

Unmanned aerial vehicle

Drone

Sees what is out of reach

Roofs, stacks, high racking, yard stock and the outer fence line are all a scaffold job or a long walk. From the air they are a scheduled flight, with the footage landing in the same place as everything else on the floor.

Autonomous, scheduled

Floor Care

Holds the floor to one standard

Sweeping and scrubbing on a fixed schedule rather than whenever somebody is free, with the route and the coverage recorded. The floor stays to the same standard on a night shift as it does on a Monday morning.
